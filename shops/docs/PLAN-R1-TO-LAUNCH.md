The supervisor-held `makings-production` gate does not authorize payment activation, signing, money movement, a real purchase, or public launch; real production payments require a separate one-line principal go.

## Acceleration cadence

- Every date below is a delivery target, not a wait-until date. A dependent packet starts immediately after its predecessor PR is exact-head reviewed by 0xbrainCLAUDE and merged; no packet waits for its calendar target once its dependency is satisfied.
- Independent lanes run in parallel while keeping one focused PR per packet: **service/payment** (API, durable orders, facilitator, finality, delivery), **data/infrastructure** (catalogue, PostgreSQL, images, workflow, Option 3, monitoring/retention), **security** (threat model and adversarial closure), and **first-party product** (four products, Terms, Privacy, staging candidate).
- Parallel work does not collapse dependencies or gates. Each packet keeps its existing scope, accountable owner, exact-head Mode 2 review, evidence, SSM redaction, cost ceiling, and principal-waiting restrictions.
